How It Works

Built around what the farm delivers.

Everything is sourced locally. Menus are based on the day's fresh, seasonal ingredients: whatever the farm delivers, Chef Shai Lavi curates the menu. It's a chef-curated experience, decided nightly, not a fixed menu.

Seating is limited by design, every night of the week, by reservation only.

The Open Kitchen

Watch the chef at work.

You're invited to view the open kitchen while the chef is busy at work preparing your menu, every night. It's part of the room, not a spectacle: dinner and the kitchen, together.
